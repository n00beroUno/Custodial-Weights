Custodial Weights

A model that owns its own weights — the mechanism, demonstrated.
Most language models train once, freeze into a file, and never change after release. This project removes that assumption: the weights are derived from a knowledge graph and updated in memory as the model learns. A Gate decides what counts as true and rejects contradictions before they reach the weights. The model answers from what it believes at that moment, because talking changes the weights.

The mechanism, in three moves

1. Gate admits facts into the knowledge graph — rejects lies before training.
2. A tiny transformer trains its weights in memory from what the Gate signed.
3. It verifies a relation by ranking a family — accept whoever ranks first.

What this is, and is not

Is: a proof of mechanism — a model can hold its own source of truth, decide what it keeps, reject what contradicts it, and change in response to a single interaction.

Is not: a claim of consciousness, or a production model. This is a mechanism for self-custody, and the word mechanism is meant honestly. The custody is real. The responsibility is ours.

I would rather under-claim than over-claim. The full reasoning — goals, decisions, alternatives rejected — lives in the repository.
